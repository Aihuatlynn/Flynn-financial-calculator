How to read the estimate

A useful planning estimate—not a guarantee of income or investment performance.

  • Income capital target: future annual income goal divided by the estimated net cash yield.
  • Capital required today: the present value of that future target using your selected net total-return assumption.
  • Monthly contribution required: the approximate monthly amount needed, alongside your current lump sum, to reach the target by your chosen date.
  • Income sustainability: a yield-based estimate; it does not guarantee that distributions will continue or that principal will retain its value.
Remember: higher yields can come with higher risks, and market returns are not smooth year to year.
